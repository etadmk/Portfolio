The schedule

Every instrument runs on the same 24 hour day. Priority decides who gets power first, not who gets the clock first.

Assumptions

Harvest is panel × peak sun hours × derate, spread across the day as a single smooth solar curve rather than a flat block.

Allocation is a priority cascade. The first instrument takes what it needs, the next takes what is left, and so on until the power runs out.

The floor check simulates charge forward as next = now + solar − standby − (duty × draw), then scales the duty cycles down until the lowest point of the day clears the floor. That is the part that makes this a scheduler rather than a calculator.

Worst case reruns the same day with the forecast uncertainty subtracted from every hour of sun. The default of 0.93 W is the verified LSTM error from the IMTA benchmark.
